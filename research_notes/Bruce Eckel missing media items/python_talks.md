## Which PyCon US (and predecessor) talks has Bruce Eckel given in every year?

### Takeaway
Five PyCon US / International Python Conference items are missing from the media file: IPC9 closing keynote "Why I Love Python" (March 2001), PyCon DC 2004 keynote "How to Argue About Types" (March 2004), PyCon DC 2005 talk "Object-Oriented Design with Python" (March 2005), PyCon US 2013 "Rethinking Errors: Learning from Scala and Go" (March 2013), and PyCon US 2026 "Demystifying the GIL" (May 2026).
He gave no talk at PyCon US 2025, and at PyCon 2016 he ran open spaces only.

### Cited Findings

#### Missing items, confirmed

1. **March 2001. "Why I Love Python" (closing keynote). Ninth International Python Conference (IPC9), Long Beach, California, March 5-8, 2001. Solo.**
   - Linux Journal's conference report lists Eckel's "Closing Keynote" with his "top ten reasons" for loving Python; conference held March 5-8, 2001 at the Long Beach Hilton. The article does not print the talk's title. [Linux Journal, "It Fits Your Brain"](https://www.linuxjournal.com/article/4731) (verified by fetch)
   - Slides titled "Why I Love Python", copyright "© 2001 Bruce Eckel MindView, Inc." on slide 1, uploaded by a third party (account "didip"); the page names no event. [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/why-i-love-python/493670) (verified by fetch)
   - Bill Venners's 2003 Artima interview series is built on the ten points of this keynote. The media file already links one part of it ("Python and the Programmer", June 2003). [Artima, Python and the Tipping Point](https://www.artima.com/intv/tipping.html) (found in search results only)
   - No recording found.

2. **March 2004. "How to Argue About Types" (keynote). PyCon DC 2004, George Washington University, Washington DC, March 24-26, 2004. Solo.**
   - An attendee's post dated March 26, 2004 (day 3) gives the keynote title as "How to Argue About Types". [yergler.net, "pycon day 3"](https://yergler.net/2004/03/26/pycon-day-3/) (verified by fetch)
   - Linux Journal lists it as "Keynote #3: Bruce Eckel", a humorous talk on static versus dynamic typing that argues a robust automated test suite is worth more than static type checks; conference dates March 24-26, 2004 at GWU. No title given there. [Linux Journal, "PyCon DC 2004"](https://www.linuxjournal.com/article/7541) (verified by fetch)
   - A February 2004 announcement describes Eckel as closing keynote, with Mitch Kapor as main keynote. [python-list, "PyCon talks and schedule posted"](https://www.python.org/pipermail/python-list/2004-February/244304.html) (found in search results only)
   - No recording found. The title rests on one attendee blog.

3. **March 2005. "Object-Oriented Design with Python" (talk). PyCon DC 2005, GWU Cafritz Conference Center (Marvin Theatre), Washington DC, March 23, 2005. Solo.**
   - Ted Leung's session notes name Bruce Eckel (Mindview, Inc.) as presenter, title "Object-Oriented Design with Python", March 23, 2005. [sauria.com session notes](https://www.sauria.com/twl/conferences/pycon2005/20050323/Object-Oriented%20Design%20with%20Python.html) (verified by fetch)

[PyCon blog, "afternoon talks"](https://pycon.blogspot.com/2005/03/afternoon-talks.html) (verified by fetch)

### Inferences
- The IPC9 keynote title "Why I Love Python" comes from the 2001-copyright slides and the Artima series, not from the Linux Journal report, so the link between title and event is strong but assembled from two sources.
- A search summary called "Why I Love Python" a "PyCon 2005-era" keynote. That is wrong: the slides are dated 2001 and Linux Journal places the top-ten keynote at IPC9 in March 2001.
- A search summary described Eckel as "a creator of the PyData community" (from Talk Python's guest page). This is almost certainly a misattribution of another guest's bio and should not be used.

### Gaps
- No official recording of "Demystifying the GIL" was found. The schedule page has no video link and search did not surface a PyCon US YouTube upload. Check the PyCon US YouTube channel and pyvideo.org's pycon-us-2026 listing by hand before publishing.
- PyCon 2007, 2008, 2009, 2011, 2012: I found no evidence of an Eckel talk or keynote (no decorators talk; his decorators material is a 2008 blog series). I did not fetch each year's full schedule, so this is absence of evidence from searches, not a schedule-by-schedule check. The PyCon 2012 "Decorators and Context Managers" entry that surfaced has no confirmed link to him.
- International Python Conference years other than IPC9 (for example IPC8 2000, IPC10 2002): nothing found; not checked exhaustively.
- Lightning talks and open spaces at PyCon US are not indexed by speaker, so any he gave are not discoverable this way.
- A wiki page "AdaptersAndInterfacesSprint" lists Eckel as coach of a sprint (likely PyCon 2005 era, given the 2005 talk's topic). I did not fetch it and cannot date it. [wiki.python.org](https://wiki.python.org/moin/AdaptersAndInterfacesSprint.html) (found in search results only)

## Which talks has he given at other Python events?

### Takeaway
Three non-US-PyCon Python events are confirmed and missing: PyConBrasil 2008 (two talks, "Why I Love Python" and "Burning Man"), EuroPython 2009 keynote(s) in Birmingham (topic "Software Archeology"), and the PyCaribbean 2017 keynote "Science is What Works".
Searches for PyCon Italia, PyCon India, PyCon Poland, PyCon Lithuania, PyCascades, PyOhio, PyTexas, PyBay, PyGotham, North Bay Python, PyData, SciPy, and PyCon Russia/CZ/Colombia/Israel returned nothing for him.

### Cited Findings

1. **September 2008. "Why I Love Python" and "Burning Man" (two talks). PyConBrasil 2008, Rio de Janeiro. Solo.**
   - Serpro (Brazilian federal IT agency) news item datelined 18 September 2008: Eckel "palestrou ontem, 18, diante de um auditório lotado no PyConBrasil 2008"; "Além de 'I love Python', Bruce Eckel apresentou, também na tarde de ontem, a palestra 'Burning Man'"; the conference ran in Rio de Janeiro through Saturday 20 September. [serpro.gov.br](https://www.serpro.gov.br/menu/noticias/noticias-antigas/a-vida-e-curta-logo-use-python) (verified by fetch)

   - The source does not call either talk a keynote. No recording found.

2. **June/July 2009. Keynote, topic "Software Archeology" (exact title unconfirmed). EuroPython 2009, Birmingham Conservatoire, Birmingham, UK. Solo.**
   - The conference site lists the keynote speakers as Bruce Eckel, Cory Doctorow, and Professor Sir Tony Hoare; event 28 June to 4 July 2009, core conference 30 June to 2 July. No keynote title given. [ep2009.europython.eu](https://ep2009.europython.eu/) (verified by fetch)

   - Michael Sparks's video roundup of 13 July 2009 lists two recordings under "Key Notes" for Bruce Eckel, with no titles: `http://europython09.blip.tv/file/2338437` and `http://europython09.blip.tv/file/2338433`. [europython mailing list](https://mail.python.org/pipermail/europython/2009-July/007488.html) (verified by fetch for the list post; the blip.tv URLs were not fetched, and blip.tv shut down in 2015, so treat them as dead)
   - Two recordings suggest two sessions (possibly two keynotes, or one keynote in two parts). Not resolved.

3. **February 2017. "Science is What Works" (keynote). PyCaribbean 2017, Puerto Rico, February 18, 2017. Solo.**
   - pyvideo.org entry "Bruce Eckel - Keynote", PyCaribbean 2017, February 18, 2017, video YouTube id _ncc69-cunA. [pyvideo.org](https://pyvideo.org/pycaribbean-2017/bruce-eckel-keynote.html) (verified by fetch)
